Child Safety Awareness

Awareness and education can help protect children from trafficking, exploitation, abuse and unsafe situations.

What is Child Trafficking?

Child trafficking involves the recruitment, transportation, transfer, harbouring or receipt of children for the purpose of exploitation. Children may be exploited through forced labour, sexual exploitation, forced begging or other harmful activities.

⚠️ Warning Signs

🚫 Fear or Anxiety

A child may appear unusually frightened, anxious or afraid to speak.

🏠 Unsafe Living Conditions

The child may live or work in unsafe or inappropriate conditions.

💼 Forced Work

A child may be forced to work for long hours or under harmful conditions.

📱 Restricted Communication

The child may have limited access to family, friends or communication devices.

How Can We Help?

  • Learn about child safety and trafficking.
  • Pay attention to possible warning signs.
  • Treat children with care and respect.
  • Never ignore a serious child safety concern.
  • Report suspected exploitation through appropriate official channels.

🌐 Online Child Safety

Children should be taught to protect their personal information online. They should avoid sharing passwords, home addresses, school details or private photographs with unknown people. Parents and guardians can help children understand safe internet practices.

Protect Every Child 🛡️

Every child deserves safety, care, education and a healthy environment to grow.
