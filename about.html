About ChildShild

ChildShild is a child protection and awareness website designed to promote the safety, care, and well-being of children.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread awareness about child safety, child trafficking, child labour, and other risks faced by children. We aim to encourage people to identify warning signs and report concerns responsibly.

Our Objectives

  • Promote awareness about child safety.
  • Provide information about child trafficking.
  • Educate people about child labour and exploitation.
  • Encourage responsible reporting of concerns.
  • Support a safer environment for children.

What ChildSafe Provides

🛡️ Child Protection

Information and awareness about protecting children from harmful situations.

📢 Awareness

Educational content about trafficking, exploitation, and child safety.

📝 Reporting

A simple interface for submitting a child safety concern.

Important Note

ChildShild is an educational college project. The reporting form is intended for demonstration purposes and is not a replacement for official emergency or child-protection services.
